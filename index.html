About me

I've been hooked on video games since I was little, starting with my very first obsession: Pokémon Red on the original Game Boy trying to collect them all or gathering all the 120 starts in Super Mario 64. That lifelong passion only grew over the years, spanning everything from falling in love all over again with the original Demon's Souls on the PS3, to diving into the latest releases of all kinds today. I’ve always had ideas for games of my own swirling around in my head. After a lifetime of dreaming about what could be, I'm finally turning those visions into reality by releasing my very own first game.

Balloon Pop

Android · Fully free, with an optional purchase to remove ads

Pop balloons against the clock, level up and unlock special balloons! You have 3 minutes to score as high as you can. Every level brings a new upgrade, and special balloons blow up, freeze, slow down or flood the sky. Compare your score on the daily leaderboard and collect achievements with Google Play Games.
